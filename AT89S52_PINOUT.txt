AT89S52 40-Pin DIP Pinout (Top View)
=====================================

        ___  ___
   P1.0 |1  \/  40| VCC (5V)
   P1.1 |2      39| P0.0
   P1.2 |3      38| P0.1
   P1.3 |4      37| P0.2
   P1.4 |5      36| P0.3
   P1.5 |6      35| P0.4
→ P1.6  |7      34| P0.5  ← MISO connects HERE (Pin 7)
   P1.7 |8      33| P0.6
   RST  |9      32| P0.7
  (RXD) |10     31| EA/VPP
  (TXD) |11     30| ALE/PROG
   P3.2 |12     29| PSEN
   P3.3 |13     28| P2.7
   P3.4 |14     27| P2.6
   P3.5 |15     26| P2.5
   P3.6 |16     25| P2.4
   P3.7 |17     24| P2.3
→ XTAL2 |18     23| P2.2  ← Crystal pin 2
→ XTAL1 |19     22| P2.1  ← Crystal pin 1
   GND  |20     21| P2.0
        ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾‾

Critical Connections for Programming:
======================================
Arduino → AT89S52
Pin 10  → Pin 9  (RESET - active HIGH for AT89S52!)
Pin 11  → Pin 6  (MOSI / P1.5)
Pin 12  → Pin 7  (MISO / P1.6)  ← YOU ARE HERE
Pin 13  → Pin 5  (SCK / P1.4)
GND     → Pin 20 (GND)
5V      → Pin 40 (VCC)

Crystal Oscillator:
===================
12MHz crystal between Pin 18 (XTAL2) and Pin 19 (XTAL1)
22pF capacitor from Pin 18 to GND (currently removed)
22pF capacitor from Pin 19 to GND (currently removed)

Other Required:
===============
Pin 31 (EA/VPP) → Connect to VCC (5V) for internal code execution
