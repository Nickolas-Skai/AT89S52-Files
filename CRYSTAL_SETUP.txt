AT89S52 Crystal Oscillator Setup
=================================

Required Components:
- 1x 12MHz crystal oscillator
- 2x 22pF ceramic capacitors (NOT 22µF, NOT 20µF!)

Connections:
- Crystal lead 1 → AT89S52 Pin 19 (XTAL1) → 22pF cap to GND
- Crystal lead 2 → AT89S52 Pin 18 (XTAL2) → 22pF cap to GND

Common mistakes:
✗ Using 20µF or 22µF capacitors (too large by ~1 billion times!)
✓ Use 22pF (picofarads) - typically small ceramic disk capacitors

Capacitor identification:
- 22pF caps are marked: "22" or "220" (220 means 22 × 10^0 = 22pF)
- 20µF caps are marked: "20µF" or "206" (much larger, electrolytic)

Alternative for testing:
- You can temporarily remove capacitors entirely to test if oscillator works
- This is not recommended for production but confirms the diagnosis
